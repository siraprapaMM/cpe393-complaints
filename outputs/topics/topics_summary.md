## Data

**BERTopic**: `paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2` embeddings of `sentence` -> UMAP (5 dims, cosine, seed 42) -> scikit-learn HDBSCAN (min cluster size 60, min samples 10); keywords by c-TF-IDF on the same aspect tokens. Raw topics: `bertopic_topics.csv`.

Both models scored with the same gensim coherence (top 10 words, same corpus and dictionary). The third row merges BERTopic down to LDA's K for a like-for-like comparison:

| model | topics | c_v | c_npmi | diversity | unassigned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LDA (K=8) | 8 | 0.4426 | 0.0042 | 1.0000 | 1.6% |
| BERTopic | 100 | 0.3780 | -0.2166 | 0.8760 | 35.4% |
| BERTopic reduced to K=8 | 8 | 0.3879 | -0.1187 | 0.9750 | 35.4% |

**Chosen model: BERTopic.** On coherence, LDA scores higher on c_v (0.4426 LDA vs 0.3780 BERTopic) and LDA on c_npmi (0.0042 vs -0.2166). Reading the topics decides it: LDA's few topics each mix several causes (e.g. taste, price and portion in one topic; staff, seating and waiting in another) and none isolates cleanliness or billing, while BERTopic gives one clean topic per cause (waiting time, rude staff, insects, dirty toilet, billing, heat / air-con, portion size, ...). Coherence counts how often top words co-occur, so it rewards LDA's frequent generic words (กิน, ทาน, อาหาร, สั่ง); on short sentences this is a known weakness of the measure.

BERTopic outliers (HDBSCAN noise): 11,764 sentences (35.4%); 0 remain after assigning each to the nearest topic (cosine similarity >= 0.3).

## Step 6 — Categories

1. **Aspect** — each of the 100 raw topics is mapped to one category by its top 10 keywords (rank-weighted match against the keyword lists in `CATEGORIES`; best score below 8 -> Other). Full mapping with matched keywords and examples: `topic_to_category.csv`.
2. **Complaint or not** — the step-3 model (SVM, word (1, 2) + char (3, 5), C=0.3), re-fitted on train, threshold -0.0118 (tuned on val by D). 9,450 of 33,246 sentences (28.4%) score as complaints and get their topic's category; the rest are "Not a complaint".

### Category descriptions (for the labeling sheet)

- **Slow service or long wait** — Waiting too long for a table, for food or for staff; slow serving.
- **Staff behavior** — Rude, careless, inattentive or unfriendly staff or owner.
- **Food taste or quality** — Taste, texture, freshness, smell or temperature of the food or drinks (bland, too sweet, tough, not fresh, smells bad, ...).
- **Price or value** — Too expensive, not worth the money, extra charges, or a portion too small for the price.
- **Cleanliness** — Dirty shop, table, dishes or toilet; insects, hair or other things that should not be there.
- **Order or billing error** — Wrong, missing or forgotten order; mistakes or problems with the bill and payment.
- **Atmosphere or facilities** — Seating, heat or air-conditioning, noise, crowding, smoke, parking and other problems with the place itself.
- **Other** — A complaint with no specific cause ("disappointed", "won't come back", "please improve") or a cause not listed above.
- **Not a complaint** — Positive or neutral sentence: praise, or a description of the shop, menu, location or visit, with no problem stated.

## Limitations to mention

- Complaint vs not is decided by a review-level classifier applied to single sentences: short or implicit complaints ("ไม่บอกว่าจองโต๊ะไหนไว้ให้") can score below the threshold and become "Not a complaint".
- Categories are assigned per topic, so a sentence that mentions two problems gets the category of the cluster it is closest to.
- Coherence favours LDA; the choice of BERTopic rests on reading the topics and on the hand-label evaluation (step 7).
- LDA is unstable here: fixing one tokenizer error (57 "มละ" tokens, see `fix_mla`) moved its best K from 5 to 8, while BERTopic's topics and categories did not change.
- "Other" is large: many complaint sentences state dissatisfaction with no cause ("ไม่ประทับใจ", "คงไม่มาอีก").
- No topic for wrong or missing orders emerged; "Order or billing error" comes from one payment topic, which also holds sentences that only mention asking for the bill ("ขอเช็คบิลดีกว่า").
- 35% of sentences were HDBSCAN noise and were given their nearest topic (`bertopic_outlier` = 1); expect more errors among them.
